# 14-day calendar (NBA)

Day 1 is the first day after the sprint changes are live. Dates are filled in then.

The "game / finding" column is blank on purpose. The live slate reaches only about a day ahead, so the game and the finding are chosen that morning from the site.

Formats:
- **Outlier**: a standing outlier. A rank at the top or bottom of the league, or one that moves sharply once the schedule is taken out.
- **Day-after**: what the numbers said beforehand, and what happened. Needs a game that finished the night before. If none did, post an outlier instead and note it.
- **Preview**: one finding about tonight's game. Four of the fourteen days. This format is being tested, not assumed.

**Friday threads.** On each Friday that falls on day 5 or later, add the weekly thread on the large NBA community, only after its rules are read. At most two in the sprint.

**Moving a post.** If a format clearly fails by day 7, swap its remaining days for the format that is working and record the change in `DECISIONS.md`.

## Daily routine (about 3 to 4 hours)

1. **Read yesterday (30 min).** Open GA4 the afternoon after each post, fill the tracker row and the daily log. Data is 12 to 48 hours late.
2. **Choose (30 min).** Open the site, choose one or two findings for today's format. Prefer a rank in the top or bottom three, or a large gap between the raw and schedule-adjusted rank.
3. **Make (45 min).** Screenshot the finding card on a phone, crop to 4:5, write the caption from `CAPTIONS.md`.
4. **Post and reply (60 min).** Post to today's channels with the tagged link, then answer every comment. Do the day's Reddit action.
5. **Talk (45 min).** One interview or one individually written feedback request.

## Rules for every post

- Every number in a post is copied from the site page it links to. Check it against the page before posting.
- Findings are chosen by hand. Nothing is scheduled to post automatically.
- Analysis, not advice. Nothing about money on a game or a result being certain, and no communities built around that.
- Team names in text only on images made for the sprint; no team logos.
- One row in `post-tracker.csv` per post, including posts that were removed.
