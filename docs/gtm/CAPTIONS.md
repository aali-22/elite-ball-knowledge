# Caption templates

Square brackets are slots. Every number in a slot is copied from the site page the post links to, as that page shows it. If the page does not show it, it does not go in the caption.

Bio line on every profile: "NBA numbers that look wrong until you see the chart. Analysis, not advice. eliteballindex.com"

## Tagged links

Tags come from a fixed list. Anything else is ignored by the site. The share button on a finding gives the link up to `?f=...`; add the tag after it with `&`.

## Format 1: standing outlier

**Instagram, single image or carousel**

> [Team] rank [N]th of 30 in [stat] as played.
> Take the schedule out and they are [M]th.
>
> [One line from the finding's own meaning, as the page words it.]
>
> Every claim on the site sits next to its chart. Link in bio.
> eliteballindex.com

Rank extreme variant:

> [Team] are [N]th of 30 in [stat]. [Other team] are [M]th of 30 in [the stat it meets].
> [The page's headline, e.g. "[N]th meets [M]th."]
>
> The chart is on the game page. Link in bio.
> eliteballindex.com

**Instagram story**: the same image, one line of text ("[N]th as played. [M]th without the schedule."), and a link sticker with the story link. Sticker text: "See the chart".

**Bluesky**

> [Team] rank [N]th of 30 in [stat] as played. Take the schedule out and they are [M]th.
>
> Chart and method: [Bluesky post link]
> eliteballindex.com

**Reddit, first comment under an original chart post** (only where the rules allow it)

> Source: computed from public box scores and play-by-play, through games of [date on the page].
> Method: every stat is placed on a league rank, then the schedule is taken out, so a team that has faced strong opponents is not marked down for it. The sentence on the chart is written by a fixed rule from those numbers, not by a person or a language model.
> Tool: my own site. The page for this chart: [Reddit comment link]
> Happy to be told where this is wrong.

**TikTok mirror**: the image as a photo post, caption "[N]th as played. [M]th without the schedule. [Team], [stat]. eliteballindex.com". No link expected.

## Format 2: day-after

Uses the line a finished game page prints under "What the numbers said before it".

**Instagram**

> Before tip-off the numbers said [Team], [P]%.
> Final: [Away] [a], [Home] [h].
>
> Score model: [right / wrong]. Matchups: [right / wrong].
> The pre-game breakdown is kept as it stood. Link in bio.
> eliteballindex.com

**Instagram story**: a screenshot of the "Final" block, one line ("What the numbers said beforehand, and what happened."), link sticker to the game page.

**Bluesky**

> Before tip-off the numbers said [Team], [P]%. Final: [Away] [a], [Home] [h]. Score model [right / wrong], matchups [right / wrong].
>
> The breakdown as it stood before the game: [Bluesky post link]
> eliteballindex.com

**Reddit comment** (in a post-game thread, only if links are allowed there; otherwise no link)

> For what it is worth, the pre-game numbers had [Team] at [P]%. One of the findings was [stat]: [Team] [N]th of 30 against [Other team] [M]th. Final was [a]-[h]. Method is rank-based with the schedule removed.

**TikTok mirror**: the same image, caption "Said beforehand: [Team], [P]%. Final: [a]-[h]. eliteballindex.com".

When the numbers were wrong, post it the same way. A record that only shows the right ones is not a record.

## Words to keep out

Anything about money on a game, prices on a game, or a result being certain. Do not call a finding a tip or a call. The site says "analysis, not advice"; the captions should read the same way.

## A clean 4:5 crop from a phone

1. Open the game page on a phone and scroll until the whole finding card is on screen, with the `eliteballindex.com` label at its foot.
2. Take a screenshot.
3. In Photos: Edit > Crop > aspect ratio > 4:5 (portrait).
4. Centre the card. The label stays inside the frame; the browser bar and the site's sticky bar stay outside it.
5. Check each number in the image against the page once more. If the page has updated since the screenshot, take it again.
6. Use the same crop for Bluesky. For a story or TikTok, crop a second copy to 9:16 and leave space top and bottom.
